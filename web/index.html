      What should I do with the result?DECISION GUIDE
      Expected net matched

      The transaction net equals your chosen expectation. Confirm invoice identity independently, along with address control and customer intent.

      Expected net differs

      Inspect the exact difference and the basis you chose. Partial payments and swept gross receipts need separate allocation; do not round the difference.

      Zero or negative net

      Review both transfer directions and the application context. This can be a normal swap, redemption or sweep; it is not an unpaid-invoice verdict.

      A receipt describes explicit movements in one transaction. It is not an account balance, payment guarantee or invoice ledger.

      What this observation cannot decideREVIEW BOUNDARIES
      1. Address control and intent: a selected address may be a wallet, exchange, router or intermediary. These events do not prove who controls it or whether a customer intended payment.
      2. Invoice allocation and reuse: a matching net does not identify an invoice, prevent reuse or authorize shipment. Apply an independent invoice ledger.
      3. Partial payments: this single-transaction review does not aggregate transactions. Enter only the expected net assigned to this transaction, not an unpaid invoice balance by assumption. The separate Reconcile orders & split payments mode sums user-assigned transaction-net observations; it does not prove commercial settlement.
      4. Sweeps and collection: a valid gross receipt can be forwarded to a controlled treasury in the same transaction. Review incoming events and controlled destinations under your own collection policy; zero net alone cannot decide invoice settlement.
      5. Mints and burns: incoming mints can increase a balance but are excluded from incoming-transfer totals. Outgoing burns reduce this net. Neither operation establishes customer intent.
      6. Balances and proof: gas is excluded; this net is not the State balance change. Evidence is one provider’s unsigned response, not a cryptographic proof.
      Reproduce four review workflows
      1. Click Direct native transfer: incoming 100.01, outgoing 0, transaction net 100.01; expected net matches. Customer intent and address ownership remain unknown.
      2. Click Exact expected-net difference: expected net 100.010001; difference 0.000001 is shown exactly. This alone is not an unpaid-invoice verdict.
      3. Click Normal swap intermediary: TakerPositionManager has 1458.033036 incoming and outgoing, net 0, with expected net left blank. Inspect the 18/6 paired logs in this normal Swap technical example.
      4. Download readable HTML and raw JSON from the direct case. Recheck saved JSON recomputes claims from saved evidence, then queries the fixed official RPC afresh; saved endpoints and conclusions are not trusted.
